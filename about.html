Why Graphotherapy & Reflection?

Handwriting is a direct neurological mirror of your subconscious mind. By making conscious micro-adjustments to specific letter strokes, we create bottom-up neurological pathways that quiet anxiety, rebuild confidence, and break persistent mental blocks.
