About the Founder

"Practical psychology should be accessible in everyday life."

I'm Manish Garg, the counsellor and graphologist behind PillowDreamWorks Foundation and author of Finding The Centre. I created this workbook and practice with a simple belief — that practical psychology tools should be accessible to everyone, not just within therapy rooms, but in everyday life.

Through psychology education, reflective practices, and compassionate guidance, this work is designed to help readers better understand themselves, build healthier habits, and create a calmer, more fulfilling life.

My Mission

"To empower individuals with practical psychology tools that make a real difference in their daily lives, and to create resources that inspire self-awareness, resilience, and lasting wellbeing."

Privacy Policy

At PillowDreamWorks (Manish Garg), we respect your personal data and privacy under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) and global standards.

1. Information Collection: We collect your name, email, phone number, and submitted handwriting samples solely for processing your orders, counselling sessions, and delivering reports.

2. Data Usage & Confidentiality: Session notes and handwriting samples are kept strictly confidential and are never sold or shared with third parties.

3. Contact: For data inquiries or deletion requests, email support@pillowdreamworks.com.

Terms of Use

Welcome to PillowDreamWorks. By using our website, purchasing our book, or booking services, you agree to these Terms of Use.

1. Service Scope: Counselling and graphotherapy sessions are educational and supportive tools. They do not constitute emergency psychiatric intervention.

2. Rescheduling Policy: Sessions can be rescheduled up to 12 hours prior to the slot. No-shows or cancellations within 12 hours are charged in full.

Refund & Replacement Policy

1. Physical Books: Damaged physical copies will be replaced free of charge within 7 days of delivery upon sending photo proof to support@pillowdreamworks.com.

2. Digital Purchases: PDF eBook downloads and digital courses are non-refundable once downloaded due to immediate file delivery.

3. Sessions & Graphotherapy: Full refunds are provided if a session is cancelled at least 24 hours in advance.

Medical & Therapeutic Disclaimer

PillowDreamWorks, Manish Garg, and CentreLine provide self-help resources, educational workbooks, graphotherapy stroke drills, and supportive 1-on-1 counselling.

Not Emergency Psychiatry: Our services do NOT diagnose medical or psychiatric disorders, nor do they replace hospital emergency care. If you are experiencing suicidal thoughts or immediate danger, call 112 immediately.
