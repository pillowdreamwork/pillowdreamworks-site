Cookie Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used by PillowDreamWorks Foundation’s website as currently implemented.

1. What we currently use

Based on the code reviewed, the site does not currently set cookies for analytics, ad targeting, remarketing, or advertising. The site does not use localStorage or sessionStorage for tracking. No consent banner is currently required for cookie-based tracking because the live code does not implement non-essential tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

2. Third-party resources

When the site loads, it may contact third-party servers to fetch scripts and fonts. These services may receive standard network information such as IP address and user-agent, in line with their own policies. The main third-party resources in the current code are:

  • Tailwind CSS CDN
  • Lucide icon CDN
  • Google Fonts

These services have their own privacy and cookie policies. Their use is not a cookie-tracking system operated by PillowDreamWorks itself.

3. Necessary vs optional

At present, the website code does not rely on non-essential cookies, advertising pixels, or tracking scripts. A cookie banner is therefore not being added at this stage because the site does not currently set optional marketing or analytics cookies that would normally require consent management.

4. User control

You can control browser cookie settings through your browser preferences. You may also block third-party scripts or clear stored browser data. If the website adds a cookie or tracking technology in future, a consent mechanism and a clear consent management system will be added before deployment.

5. Changes

If the site begins using analytics, ad technology, a payment processor, or a CRM that sets cookies or stores data, we will update this policy and implement the appropriate consent mechanism before enabling those technologies.

6. Contact

For questions about cookies or website data handling, contact support@pillowdreamworks.com.

This is a technical and operational summary, not a legal opinion. Regulatory requirements may vary by jurisdiction and by the exact services used in future site deployments.
