Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy applies to session bookings, service requests, and digital products as listed on the site.

Refund and cancellation terms may vary based on the product or service booked. The site does not currently use a live payment processor; therefore, the exact refund mechanics may depend on the final delivery method selected by the business.

1. Session cancellations

For counselling, crisis support, or graphotherapy bookings, clients may be eligible for rescheduling or a refund if they cancel within the timing communicated during booking. No-show or late cancellation policies should be confirmed at the time of booking and in the confirmation message sent by the business.

2. Digital products

Digital downloads, PDFs, and immediate-access digital offerings may be non-refundable once delivered or downloaded, unless the business agrees otherwise in writing. The site currently presents some digital products as non-refundable after access is granted.

3. Physical products

For physical books or workbooks, damaged items may be eligible for replacement or refund if the issue is reported with supporting evidence within the time period stated on the relevant listing. This should be confirmed directly with the business before a claim is made.

4. Assessment or consultation requests

If a booking request is cancelled before the session begins, the amount may be refunded or credited according to the business’s communication with the client. Fees paid for services that have already started or been completed are generally not refundable.

5. How to request a refund or cancellation

To ask about a refund, reschedule, or a cancellation, contact the support email below and include the booking name, date, service, and reason. If you are using a payment processor later, that processor’s own refund rules may also apply.

6. Contact

For refund or cancellation questions, contact support@pillowdreamworks.com.

This document is intended to reflect the current public site wording and operational processes. If the business introduces a real payment gateway or a different contractual model, the final refund policy should be reviewed and revised in line with the actual processor and service terms.
