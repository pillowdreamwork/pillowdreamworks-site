Chapter 1: Response Agility Page 18

Trigger to Response Planner

Become Aware • Pause • Choose Healthier Response

1. The Trigger: What external event or thought sparked the reaction?
2. The Automatic Urge: To yell, withdraw, or scroll mindlessly?
3. The 10-Second Pause: Take 3 slow diaphragmatic breaths.
4. Chosen Response: What aligns with my values and dignity?
Between stimulus and response, there is a space. In that space is our power.
