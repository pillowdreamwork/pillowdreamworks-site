Form Consent & Privacy Notice

When you submit a contact, booking, assessment, or order form on this website, you are confirming that the information you provide is accurate and that you are authorising the business to use it to respond to your request.

What the form is for

Form information may be used to respond to a general inquiry, schedule a counselling session, confirm a booking, share a workbook or digital product delivery message, or support assessment booking requests.

What information is needed

We only request the information necessary to contact you and complete the relevant service request. This usually includes name, email, phone number, service interest, and message details. For assessment intake, age and gender may be requested for the self-assessment experience.

How we use it

Your details are used to respond to your request, coordinate session or order communication, and provide the specific service or product you requested. Where contact is made via email or WhatsApp, the receiving platform’s own terms and privacy rules also apply.

Consent

By checking the consent box on a form, you are confirming that you agree to the use of your information for the purpose stated on that form. We do not pre-check consent. We do not sell personal data. The site does not currently implement a tracking-based marketing or analytics consent system, because the reviewed code does not use marketing trackers.

Important

These forms are not emergency channels. If you are in immediate danger or crisis, contact a local emergency service or call 112 immediately.

Contact

For questions, contact support@pillowdreamworks.com.

This notice reflects the current site design and implementation as reviewed. If the website later adds a backend, CRM, cloud form processor, or analytics provider, the consent and retention wording will be revised to match the actual system before launch.
